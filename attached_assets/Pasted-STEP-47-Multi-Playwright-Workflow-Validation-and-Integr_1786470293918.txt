1. Validate Playwright Selection

- each playwright can be selected independently;

- All playwrights selects all eight;
- deselecting one playwright from All leaves the other seven selected;
- the app cannot enter a zero-playwright state;
- changing selections updates the active corpus immediately;
- no stale results remain from the previous playwright selection.

Do not change checkbox behaviour unless a bug is found.

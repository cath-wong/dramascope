STEP 33 — Conventionalisation Indicators

Goal

Implement the first interpretive layer of the Semantic Dashboard.

This step evaluates expression candidates and expression families using transparent, corpus-derived indicators of conventionalisation.

The dashboard must NOT claim that expressions are formulaic, fossilised, or entrenched.

Instead, it should identify expressions and expression families that exhibit stronger or weaker evidence of conventionalisation.

This section therefore provides evidence, not conclusions.

Section C — Conventionalisation


Analytical Principle

Section C should not classify expressions as "formulaic".

Instead it evaluates several observable indicators.

Interpretation remains with the researcher.


Indicators

Reuse the existing expression candidates and expression families.

Do NOT regenerate expressions.

For every expression family calculate:


1.

Family Frequency

Already available.

Definition:

Total frequency of all member expressions.


--------------------------------------------------


2.

Family Size

Already available.

Definition:

Number of distinct member expressions.


--------------------------------------------------


3.

Representative Dominance

Definition:

Percentage of total family frequency contributed by the representative expression.

Example

good my lord

frequency = 80

family total = 100

dominance = 80%

Higher dominance indicates greater structural stability.

Do NOT describe this as entrenchment.


--------------------------------------------------


4.

Average Member Frequency

Definition:

Family Frequency

divided by

Family Size

Higher values indicate stronger recurrence across members.


--------------------------------------------------


5.

Lexical Diversity

Definition:

Number of unique member expressions.

This may equal Family Size initially.

Include anyway because later versions may distinguish between types.


--------------------------------------------------


Summary Score

Do NOT call this a Formulaicity Score.

Call it:

Conventionalisation Indicator

Compute a simple transparent index.

Suggested equally weighted components:

- normalized family frequency
- representative dominance
- average member frequency

Scale:

0–100

Do NOT over-engineer.

The score is an exploratory indicator only.

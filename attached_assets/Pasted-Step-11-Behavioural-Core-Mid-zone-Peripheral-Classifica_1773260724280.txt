Step 11 — Behavioural Core / Mid-zone / Peripheral Classification

Scope Statement

Implement ONLY Step 11 inside the Discursive Concepts tab.

This step updates the quad classification rule used in the
Core vs Peripheral Quads panel so that the three classes reflect
discursive behaviour rather than a median-based threshold.

The goal is to ensure that a genuine Mid-zone category appears
for quads that are repeated but not yet discursively stable.

- CSV export behaviour

Goal

Replace the current median-based classification rule with a
behavioural LDNA-style rule that produces three meaningful classes.

New behavioural rule:

Core
frequency > 1
AND
slices ≥ 2

Peripheral
frequency = 1
AND
slices = 1

Mid-zone
all remaining quads

Interpretation:

Core = repeated and distributed across discourse slices

Mid-zone = repeated but confined to a narrow discursive context

Peripheral = single occurrence within a single slice

This rule removes the dependency on the median frequency and
produces a stable middle category.

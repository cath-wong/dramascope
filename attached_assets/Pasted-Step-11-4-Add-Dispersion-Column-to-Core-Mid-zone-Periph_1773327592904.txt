client/src/pages/Analysis.tsx

- the raw number, or
- a short label like `3 slices`

Choose the smallest implementation that fits the existing table style.

6. Do not remove the existing `Slices Present` field from the exported dataset.

8. If sorting is already supported in this table and can safely be enabled for the new column with a minimal change, it may sort by `slices_present`.

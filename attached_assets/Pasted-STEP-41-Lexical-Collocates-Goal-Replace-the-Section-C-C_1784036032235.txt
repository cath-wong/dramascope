Analytical Definition

A collocate is an individual token occurring within a configurable number of tokens to the left or right of the selected word.

For each collocate, calculate:

- total co-occurrence frequency
- left frequency
- right frequency
- corpus frequency
- LogDice association score

Use LogDice as the principal association measure because it is relatively interpretable and less strongly biased towards very rare words than raw Mutual Information.

Formula:

LogDice = 14 + log2(
  2 × cooccurrenceFrequency
  /
  (nodeFrequency + collocateFrequency)
)

Do not claim that a high LogDice score proves semantic relatedness. It indicates a stronger corpus association under the current settings.
